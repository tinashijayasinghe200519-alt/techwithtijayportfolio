About me

Hi, I'm Tinashi — the person behind Tech with Tijay.

I'm a first-year university student with a growing interest in IT, cloud computing and the systems that quietly run modern life. Tech with Tijay started as a way to write down what I learn as I learn it — partly for myself, partly for anyone else figuring out the same things.

Outside of coursework, I read and write about cloud platforms, digital marketing, computational thinking, and the occasional detour into Sri Lankan heritage and travel. This site is where all of that comes together: honest write-ups, useful resources, and a help desk for fellow IT students.

Before you ask

Quick answers

How do I access the study materials on Google Drive?+

Click "Open Google Drive" above. You may need to request access the first time — that request comes straight to me and is usually approved quickly.

Do I need to subscribe to the YouTube channel to watch tutorials?+

No — all videos are public. Subscribing just means you'll be notified when a new tutorial goes up.

What if I can't find an answer to my question?+

Head to the Contact page and send a message. I read every one and reply by email, usually within a few days.

Is Tech with Tijay only for IT students?+

The help desk is built with IT students in mind, but the blog and articles cover topics anyone curious about technology can follow.
